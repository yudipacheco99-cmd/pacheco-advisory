Proprietary framework

The PAC BUILD™ Methodology.

Five deliberate phases move the company from uncertainty to a documented, operating finance function the team can own.

B
Phase 01 · Baseline

Assess the current state.

Map accounts, processes, roles, systems, and obligations. Document what exists, where it breaks, and what creates exposure.

U
Phase 02 · Unify

Design one connected system.

Architect accounting, cost tracking, banking, expense management, controls, and reporting to work together—not as isolated tools.

I
Phase 03 · Implement

Build and activate the workflows.

Clean the books, structure payroll, organize compliance, configure workflows, and test the system against real operating conditions.

L
Phase 04 · Lead

Transfer the operating knowledge.

Walk the responsible team through every process, decision point, report, and control until the work can continue without outside dependence.

D
Phase 05 · Drive

Hand over a system the client owns.

Complete documentation, establish review rhythms, and transfer the keys. Independence is the measure of a successful engagement.

Representative challenge 01Compliance & controls

Compliance without operational gridlock.

Construction owners may not realize how payroll and labor obligations connect across the IRS, Texas Workforce Commission, Texas Comptroller, and other authorities. The challenge is building controls that support compliance without slowing the field or burdening daily operations.

ScopeLabor compliance, payroll processes, documentation, and internal controls
WorkMap connected obligations, rebuild approval and recordkeeping processes, train the team, and flag decisions requiring CPA or construction-counsel review
TimingInitial process rebuild often begins within the first two weeks, subject to scope and record condition
Controls designed to protect the business and keep work moving.Practical procedures that support compliance while respecting operational reality.
Representative challenge 02Banking & expenses

Separate the money. Connect the systems.

Funds and expenses can become commingled as a construction company grows. Owners need a banking structure suited to the size of the business, expense controls that teams can follow, and platforms that connect cleanly with accounting while supporting future financing needs.

ScopeBanking architecture, expense controls, accounting integration, and financing readiness
WorkRecommend the account structure, evaluate proven platforms, connect approvals and expense data, and guide appropriate banking relationships
TimingCore recommendations and implementation roadmap typically developed in one to two weeks
Clearer cash visibility with a structure built for control and growth.A coordinated banking, expense, and accounting environment—not another disconnected tool.
Representative challenge 03Field to finance

Turn field activity into cost, evidence, and cash.

Hours, daily progress, material delays, blocked access, scope changes, approvals, and photos often remain in messages or conversations. Without a structured path into finance, costs become harder to explain, bill, collect, and manage.

ScopeProject activation, crew documentation, job costing, billing support, and management review
WorkBuild the workflow from approved scope and job number through daily field evidence, PM validation, project cost, invoice support, and collection follow-up
ControlNo payment without approved support; no project cost without a job number; no change or delay left undocumented
Problems become visible while there is still time to act.Weekly plan-versus-actual review connects labor, margin, billing, collection, and the next responsible action.

Before we speak

Clear expectations from the start.

A focused engagement begins with fit, scope, and an honest view of the work.

Do you work with established construction companies?

Yes. Established companies often engage Pacheco & Advisory Co. when growth has exposed control gaps, reporting is no longer decision-ready, multiple entities or systems need integration, the finance team needs stronger leadership, or ownership is preparing for financing, acquisition, or another major transition.

Can you support an acquisition or post-acquisition integration?

Yes. Support can include financial due diligence assistance, historical-book cleanup, opening-balance validation, account and close standardization, compliance review, multi-entity and intercompany design, reporting alignment, controls, and team transition. Legal, tax, valuation, and assurance matters remain with the client’s appropriately licensed advisors.

Do you represent investors on luxury construction projects?

Yes. Support can be structured as investor-side owner’s representation and development oversight. The work may include acting as the communication link between investor and builder, monitoring budgets, draws, cost-to-complete, documentation, changes, milestones, and project reporting, and helping the investor surface questions or variances requiring action. This advisory role does not replace the architect, engineer, general contractor, attorney, broker, or other licensed project professionals.

Can you provide outsourced or fractional financial leadership?

Yes. Support can be structured as a defined transformation engagement, interim or fractional Controller/CFO-level leadership, or selective senior advisory. The scope can strengthen an existing department, lead a transition, build executive reporting, or guide major decisions without requiring the company to add a full-time executive immediately.

Is this bookkeeping or an outsourced accounting department?

The primary work is higher-level financial infrastructure, controls, reporting, compliance, integration, and decision support. We can lead or strengthen an outsourced finance function when scoped appropriately, but routine transaction processing is not the defining value of the engagement.

Do we need clean books before starting?

No. The baseline assessment is designed to identify what is reliable, what needs correction, and what must be rebuilt. The condition of the records affects scope, but it does not prevent a productive first conversation.

Which engagement should I choose?

Established companies can begin with PAC BUILD™ when the transformation need is clear, or with the PAC Financial Audit™ when leadership wants an independent diagnosis first. The PAC Advisory Circle™ provides selective senior oversight for qualified companies. PAC Foundation™ serves entrepreneurs establishing the business correctly from the start.

Can you work with our existing bookkeeper or internal team?

Yes. Engagements are designed to clarify roles and strengthen the people who will operate the system. Existing team members can be trained into documented workflows rather than displaced by an indefinite outside dependency.

Are services available in Spanish?

Yes. Discovery, implementation, documentation discussions, and team training are available in English and Spanish. Todos los servicios están disponibles en inglés y en español.
