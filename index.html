Our Approach

We are not here to keep you dependent on us.

Most financial consulting firms are built on dependency. The longer you need them, the more they bill. Pacheco & Advisory Co. is built on the opposite principle.

I.

We Are Builders, Not Keepers.

Our primary role is to design and build your financial infrastructure from the ground up — the systems, the controls, the compliance framework. We do not come to maintain what already exists. We come to build what should.

II.

We Teach, Not Just Do.

Every system we build, we teach. Every process we design, we document and walk through with the people who will run it. Our engagements end with your team capable — not dependent on us.

III.

We Serve Construction — Exclusively.

From the entrepreneur registering their first LLC to the established contractor preparing for acquisition. One industry. Complete depth at every stage of the construction business lifecycle.

IV.

We Stay at the Level That Matters.

For clients who want us in their corner after the build, the PAC Advisory Circle™ provides quarterly audits and strategic oversight — by invitation, after engagement completion. Not a bookkeeping retainer.

Gloria Yudibeth Pacheco
Founder & Principal, Pacheco & Advisory Co.

Yudibeth's career has been built entirely within the construction industry. Over more than 8 years of progressive executive leadership, she has served as the principal financial officer for high-growth construction companies, built accounting departments from zero, led M&A financial integrations for acquired entities, and held simultaneous CFO and Operations Manager responsibility — managing both the financial reporting and the daily operational decisions of construction businesses generating up to $12M in annual revenue.

Her industry depth spans four construction verticals: general construction, luxury residential real estate and spec home development in the $3M–$10M range, restoration and remodeling, and commercial roofing. In each, she has operated from the inside — not as an outside observer, but as the executive making the financial decisions, managing the banking relationships, designing the cost systems, and building the compliance infrastructure.

Her regulatory expertise covers the full government environment of Texas construction and small business: IRS tax obligations, Texas Workforce Commission requirements, Texas Franchise Tax, business registration and entity formation, certified payroll compliance, sales tax administration, and subcontractor classification.

Through the proprietary PAC BUILD™ methodology and a four-tier engagement model, she serves construction companies at every stage — from the entrepreneur registering their first business entity to the established contractor preparing for acquisition.
